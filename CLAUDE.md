# Design System: "Am I Cooked?" (Survival HUD)

## 1. Tokens
### Neutrals
- `bg-deep`: `#0D0D0D` (Obsidian)
- `bg-surface`: `#1A1A1E` (Muted Slate)
- `text-primary`: `#F2F2F4` (Off-white)
- `text-mute`: `#8B8B94` (Slate Grey)

### Mood Signal Colors
- `mood-comfortable`: `#00FF9F` (Neon Mint)
- `mood-sweating`: `#FDFD96` (Warning Yellow)
- `mood-cooked`: `#FF3131` (Panic Red)

### Typography
- **Display:** `JetBrains Mono` (Survivalist/Dev vibe)
- **Body:** `Public Sans` (High-contrast legibility)
- **Numbers:** Tabular figures (JetBrains Mono)
- **Scale:** Hero (8-10x body), Body (14px min)

### Spacing & Radius
- `radius-sm`: `4px` (Harder, mechanical edges)
- `radius-lg`: `12px` (Limited use)
- `gap-base`: `1rem`

### Motion
- `ease`: `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)`
- `duration`: `200ms`
